## Design Context

**Register:** `product` — design serves a workflow tool (inventory + sales management). Clarity, speed, and data density are the priorities.

**North Star:** "The Control Room" — operators need situational awareness over a complex business. Every screen should feel decisive, dense where density is warranted, and never decorative for its own sake.

**Brand personality:** Confident, modern, reliable.

**Primary color:** Command Blue (`oklch(0.55 0.2 250)`) — used only for interactive elements and active states. Its rarity is its authority.

**Key design principles:**
1. Dual-persona density — owner screens scan-rich; staff screens action-first
2. Status is never ambiguous — color + icon + label, always together
3. Speed over decoration — high-frequency workflows (sales, stock entry) must be minimum taps
4. Earned polish — refinement that improves comprehension, not ornamentation
5. Touch-tolerant without compromise — 44px minimum touch targets throughout (desktop + tablet)
